## Changes since 0.3.0

Scene-cloud recipe (no circle bubbles); a reference's style overrides the
volume and texture rules and applies to every element; finish step for
detail; text fitting that works in `rsvg-convert` (no `textLength` or
`textPath` reliance); pose mapping from reference to canvas; poster or
print asset row; flat-area color sampling; tube, leaf, rock-field, and
curved-text recipes; repair order and a soft budget of about 6 renders per
asset; up to 4 gradients for glossy products; 50% overlay compare.
